# Lecture slides

The shared language for the bioinformatics lecture decks and the HEBMU slide theme.

## Language

**Repeated pattern**:
A visual treatment that appears on more than one slide.
_Avoid_: utility, snippet, component

**One-off**:
A visual treatment that appears on a single slide.
_Avoid_: custom slide, escape hatch

**Crowded slide**:
A slide whose content does not fit at the normal reading size.
_Avoid_: dense slide, small-text slide

**Compact density**:
The tighter reading size of a crowded slide.
_Avoid_: text shrink, small font

**Bilingual heading**:
A slide title that carries the Chinese name and the English name in one line.
_Avoid_: subtitle, bilingual title

**Neutral card**:
A box that holds one item among parallel items, and makes no claim about importance or risk.
_Avoid_: colored card, info box

**Callout**:
A box that makes a claim the listener should not miss.
_Avoid_: alert, banner, colored box

**Warning**:
A callout that limits how a fact should be used.
_Avoid_: note, tip

**Key point**:
A callout that states a fact, or a set of criteria, the lecture wants remembered.
_Avoid_: summary, highlight

**Check**:
A callout that asks the listener to reason before the next slide.
_Avoid_: quiz, question box

**Procedure**:
A callout that lists actions in the order they are performed.
_Avoid_: pipeline, workflow

**Two-column**:
A slide body split into two equal columns.
_Avoid_: grid, side-by-side

**Source line**:
A short muted line that states where a number, figure, or statement came from.
_Avoid_: caption, footnote

**Diagram look**:
The color and type of a diagram's boxes and arrows.
_Avoid_: chart style, theme

**Diagram layout**:
The spacing of diagram boxes and the path of the lines between them.
_Avoid_: curve, routing
